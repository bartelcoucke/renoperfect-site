Water, storm and fire damage

Urgent help for private owners, property managers, insurers and architects

Damage after a storm or a fire, a damp problem that keeps coming back, moisture seeping in through the façade, or roof trouble after heavy rain: call us for an emergency call-out.

We carry out the emergency repair to stop things getting worse, then the permanent repair — insurance claim included, from start to finish.

  • Repairing leaks in water mains, drains, heating pipes and gas pipes
  • Condensation problems, insulation faults and roof trouble such as a leak
  • Rising damp, moisture seepage and water ingress
  • Water and storm damage, with the insurance claim handled in full
  • Repairing fences blown over by the wind
  • Hail damage to conservatories with polycarbonate sheets
  • Repair of consequential damage (internal walls, plastering, painting)
  • Drying out and rebuilding after water, storm and fire damage
